My VR Media Player

All formats, all projections, one lightweight player. An offline-first local VR video player for Android — your videos never leave your device.

Offline-first playback 360° / 180° / Fisheye / Flat SBS / Top-Bottom stereo libVLC + MediaPlayer engines Cardboard headsets
Get it on Google Play Privacy Policy

Package: com.solusvoid.myvrplayer · Requires Android 7.0+ · Free with ads, optional one-time Remove Ads.

Features

Plays anything, offline

Browse videos from your device with fast thumbnails, folders, search and sort. Airplane mode = playback still works. No account, no cloud, no streaming.

True VR projections

Spherical 360°, 180° hemisphere, fisheye dome (120–280°), flat perspective, and 360 cube (EAC) — with Mono, Side-by-Side and Top-Bottom stereo for headsets.

Head tracking & gestures

Rotation-vector sensor fusion, touch drag (magic window), pinch-to-zoom FOV, recenter with double-tap, and orientation lock.

Two decoder engines

libVLC (recommended, plays all formats with hardware decoding) or the lightweight built-in MediaPlayer. Switch anytime in Settings.

Your history stays yours

Resume positions and preferences live only on your device. History is never uploaded and is excluded from cloud backup.

Ads & Premium

This app contains ads (Google AdMob). Ads appear only on the browser and settings screens — never during video playback or in VR/headset mode.

An optional one-time Remove Ads purchase (remove_ads_premium) disables all ads and unlocks full resolution for high-resolution videos. Network access is used only for ads; if ads fail to load, playback continues normally.

2. Offline-first

The app plays video files stored on your device. Your videos are never uploaded, streamed, or sent to our servers — there are no accounts and no cloud sync. The only server-side component is a stateless purchase-verification service that stores nothing (see Privacy §8). Playback history and settings stay on your device and are excluded from cloud backup.

3. Ads

The app contains ads (Google AdMob). Ads appear only on the browser and settings screens — never during video playback or in VR/headset mode. Network access is used only for ads and the one-time purchase verification; if ads fail to load (for example in airplane mode), playback continues normally.

4. Remove Ads purchase

An optional one-time purchase (remove_ads_premium) via Google Play Billing permanently disables all ads and unlocks full resolution for high-resolution videos on the purchasing Google account. Purchases are verified server-side through a stateless SolusVoid service to prevent fake-billing apps (see Privacy §8). Payment, refunds, and revocations are handled entirely by Google Play under the Google Play Terms.

5. Safety

Take breaks during VR use. Stop immediately if you feel nausea, dizziness, or eye strain. Keep volume at safe levels. Do not use VR headsets while driving, walking near traffic, or in any situation requiring full attention. The app is provided “as is”, without warranties to the extent permitted by law.

1. Playback engines in the current app

Android MediaPlayer / MediaCodec

The default engine uses the Android platform's MediaPlayer and device codecs. These are provided by the operating system, not bundled third-party decoders.

AndroidX Media3 ExoPlayer — androidx.media3:media3-exoplayer:1.11.1

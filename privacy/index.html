1. Overview — your videos stay on your device

My VR Media Player is an offline-first local video player. It plays video files stored on your device (via the Android media library). Your videos are never uploaded, streamed, or sent to our servers — there are no accounts and no cloud sync.

Internet access in the app is used only for advertising (Google AdMob), the one-time Remove Ads purchase (Google Play Billing), and purchase verification via a stateless SolusVoid service that stores nothing (see §8). No analytics, no accounts, no cloud sync. If ads fail to load — for example in airplane mode — video playback continues normally, and a previously purchased upgrade stays honored on your device.

2. Data we collect

We do not operate our own analytics or tracking. The following data is processed by Google's advertising and store SDKs embedded in the app (Google Mobile Ads / AdMob, User Messaging Platform for consent, Google Play Billing, and Google Play Integrity) plus the stateless purchase-verification flow described in §8. The app does not embed a third-party crash-reporting SDK; diagnostics come from the ads SDK and Google Play (for example Play Vitals), and from reports you choose to send us.

IP address / approximate locationIP address is processed by the ads SDK to deliver ads, estimate coarse location for ad targeting/fraud prevention, and diagnose failures. In Play Data safety this is declared under Approximate location (and related device/other identifiers), not as a standalone "IP address" category.
Advertising ID (Device or other IDs)The Android advertising ID (resettable by you) used for ad personalization, frequency capping, and measurement. In Play Data safety this is declared under Device or other IDs. You can reset or delete it in Android Settings (see §4).
App interactionsAd views, ad clicks/taps, and purchase confirmation for Remove Ads. Used for ad delivery, billing entitlement, and declared under App interactions in Play Data safety.
Purchase historyWhen you buy or restore Remove Ads, the app sends the Google Play purchase token (and, on release builds, a Google Play Integrity token about the app/device verdict) to a stateless SolusVoid verification service, which checks it with Google's Play Developer API and Play Integrity API and returns a signed result. These tokens are processed transiently to confirm the purchase and detect fake billing or tampered app copies; the service does not write them to a database. Google Play retains purchase records under Google's terms. In Play Data safety this is declared under Purchase history.
DiagnosticsDiagnostic and performance data from the Google Mobile Ads SDK and Google Play (such as Play Vitals), used to detect failures and improve stability. The app does not embed Crashlytics and does not upload your video files; reports you send voluntarily are used only to investigate the issue you report.

Data we do NOT collect

We do not collect your name, email, phone number, precise location, contacts, media file contents, or playback history. Playback history and per-video playback state are stored only locally on your device and are excluded from Android backup and device transfer. General app settings also live on the device and are local to the installation.

3. How we use data

  • Advertising: display banner, native, interstitial, and rewarded ads; cap frequency; detect invalid traffic/fraud.
  • Measurement: understand aggregate ad performance (impressions, clicks).
  • Purchases: verify (server-side, to prevent fake-billing apps) and remember the one-time Remove Ads purchase on your Google account.
  • Stability: diagnose crashes and keep playback reliable.

We do not sell personal data. We do not use your videos or local history for advertising.

4. Consent & advertising choices (UMP)

On first launch the app requests consent information via Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) and shows a consent form where required (EEA/UK/Switzerland and applicable US states). Ads are requested only after the consent check allows it. If you decline personalized ads where offered, the app shows non-personalized ads or no ads request at all — playback is unaffected.

Your device-level choices, available anytime:

  • Reset or delete your Advertising ID: Android Settings → Privacy → Ads → Reset advertising ID or Delete advertising ID.
  • Opt out of ad personalization: Android Settings → Privacy → Ads → Opt out of Ads Personalization (wording varies by version).
  • Manage advertising choices: in the app, open Settings → Legal → Privacy options when a consent form is required; the form is also shown before ads are requested. You can also reset or delete your Advertising ID and opt out of ad personalization in Android Settings.
  • Remove all ads: buy the one-time Remove Ads upgrade (see §8) — with premium status, the app performs no ad initialization, preload, or display.

Google's use of data for ads is governed by Google's Privacy Policy and Google's advertising disclosures.

5. Sharing & third parties

Data described in §2 is processed by Google (Google Mobile Ads / AdMob, UMP consent SDK, Google Play Billing, Google Play Integrity, and Google Play diagnostics) to provide ads, purchases, verification, and stability — and by the stateless SolusVoid verification service (§8), which does not store purchase payloads. The website is served by GitHub Pages, and the verification service runs on Cloudflare Workers; those providers process technical data under their own terms and privacy policies. We embed no other third-party analytics, social, or advertising SDKs. Open-source attribution: Licenses. App terms: Terms of Use.

6. Security & storage

Ad, diagnostic, and purchase-verification data in transit is protected with TLS encryption, and the verification service does not store purchase or integrity payloads in a database. Your video files, playback history, and preferences remain on your device; the local history database and the encrypted entitlement file are explicitly excluded from Android backup and device transfer. The public website is hosted by GitHub Pages, which records visitor IP addresses for security; the verification service is hosted on Cloudflare Workers. Those providers may keep operational logs under their own policies.

7. Retention & deletion

  • Advertising/diagnostic data is retained according to Google's retention schedules (see Google's policy linked above).
  • Local data you control: clear the app's local data from Android Settings → Apps → My VR Media Player → Storage, or uninstall the app.
  • Clear history: Settings → Playback history → Clear history deletes saved playback history and per-video settings. Your video files, app settings, and the Remove Ads entitlement are kept; the current video keeps its display settings, and continuing playback starts new history.
  • Deletion requests: because we hold no server-side account data, email solusvoidofficial@gmail.com for privacy questions and we will direct you to the right Google control (Advertising ID reset/deletion, Play purchase records).

8. Remove Ads (one-time purchase)

The app offers a single one-time purchase, remove_ads_premium, via Google Play Billing. It permanently disables all ads and removes the app's resolution limit for high-resolution videos on the purchasing Google account. It does not guarantee that a device can decode every format or resolution; actual playback depends on the device's codec and GPU capabilities and on the settings you choose. The entitlement is stored encrypted on your device (usable offline) and re-verified online through a stateless SolusVoid verification service, which validates the purchase with Google Play's Developer API and — on release builds — Google Play Integrity, in order to detect fake billing and tampered app copies. Refunds/revocations handled by Google Play remove the entitlement. Payment processing is handled entirely by Google Play under the Google Play Terms.

9. App permissions

Read media (video)To list and play your local video files. Required for the core feature.
Internet + network stateAds and purchase verification. Loading and displaying AdMob ads and consent messaging, plus the one-time Remove Ads verification (§8). Playback works without connectivity.
Advertising ID permissionTo read the resettable advertising ID for ads (see §4).
BillingGoogle Play Billing is used to offer and verify the one-time Remove Ads purchase; it does not add a special Android runtime permission. The verification step sends the purchase token (and a Play Integrity token on release builds) to the stateless SolusVoid service described in §8.
Screen-on during playbackNo wake-lock permission is requested. The app keeps the screen on with a window flag while a video is playing.

10. Children

The app is intended for users aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. The Play listing declares the target age groups and content rating (including the presence of ads). A target age of 13+ does not by itself remove legal obligations that apply in a user's country.

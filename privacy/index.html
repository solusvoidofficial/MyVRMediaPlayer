1. Overview — your videos stay on your device

My VR Media Player is an offline-first local video player. It plays video files stored on your device (via the Android media library). Your videos are never uploaded, streamed, or sent to our servers — there are no accounts and no cloud sync.

Internet access in the app is used only for advertising (Google AdMob), the one-time Remove Ads purchase (Google Play Billing), and purchase verification via a stateless SolusVoid service that stores nothing (see §8). No analytics, no accounts, no cloud sync. If ads fail to load — for example in airplane mode — video playback continues normally, and a previously purchased upgrade stays honored on your device.

2. Data we collect

We do not operate our own analytics or tracking. The following data is collected by Google's advertising and store SDKs embedded in the app (Google Mobile Ads / AdMob, User Messaging Platform for consent, Google Play Billing, Google Play Integrity, and crash reporting) plus the stateless purchase-verification flow described in §8:

IP addressCollected by the ads SDK to deliver ads, estimate coarse location for ad targeting/fraud prevention, and diagnose failures. This matches the Play Data Safety disclosure "IP address — collected".
Advertising IDThe Android advertising ID (resettable by you) used for ad personalization, frequency capping, and measurement. Matches "Device / advertising ID — collected". You can reset or delete it in Android Settings (see §4).
Product interactionsAd views, ad clicks/taps, and purchase confirmation for Remove Ads. Used for ad delivery, billing entitlement, and the Play Data Safety disclosure "App interactions — collected".
Purchase verificationWhen you buy or restore Remove Ads, the app sends the Google Play purchase token (and, on release builds, a Google Play Integrity token about the app/device verdict) to a stateless SolusVoid verification service, which checks it with Google's Play Developer API and Play Integrity API and returns a signed result. These tokens are processed transiently to confirm the purchase and detect fake billing or tampered app copies — nothing is stored server-side.
DiagnosticsCrash logs and performance data used to fix bugs. Matches "Crash logs / diagnostics — collected". Crash logs never include your video file contents.

Data we do NOT collect

We do not collect your name, email, phone number, precise location, contacts, media file contents, or playback history. Playback history and settings (last position, projection, favorites) are stored only locally on your device and are excluded from cloud backup.

3. How we use data

  • Advertising: display banner, native, interstitial, and rewarded ads; cap frequency; detect invalid traffic/fraud.
  • Measurement: understand aggregate ad performance (impressions, clicks).
  • Purchases: verify (server-side, to prevent fake-billing apps) and remember the one-time Remove Ads purchase on your Google account.
  • Stability: diagnose crashes and keep playback reliable.

We do not sell personal data. We do not use your videos or local history for advertising.

4. Consent & advertising choices (UMP)

On first launch the app requests consent information via Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) and shows a consent form where required (EEA/UK and applicable US states). Ads are requested only after the consent check allows it. If you decline personalized ads where offered, the app shows non-personalized ads or no ads request at all — playback is unaffected.

Your device-level choices, available anytime:

  • Reset or delete your Advertising ID: Android Settings → Privacy → Ads → Reset advertising ID or Delete advertising ID.
  • Opt out of ad personalization: Android Settings → Privacy → Ads → Opt out of Ads Personalization (wording varies by version).
  • Manage consent in-app: where the consent form is available in your region, you can revisit choices from the app's consent entry point.
  • Remove all ads: buy the one-time Remove Ads upgrade (see §8) — with premium status, the app performs no ad initialization, preload, or display.

Google's use of data for ads is governed by Google's Privacy Policy and Google's advertising disclosures.

5. Sharing & third parties

Data described in §2 is processed by Google (AdMob / Google Mobile Ads SDK, UMP consent SDK, Google Play Billing, Google Play Integrity, crash reporting) to provide ads, purchases, verification, and stability — and by the stateless SolusVoid verification service (§8), which stores nothing. We embed no other third-party analytics, social, or advertising SDKs. Open-source attribution: Licenses. App terms: Terms of Use.

6. Security & storage

Ad, diagnostic, and purchase-verification data in transit is protected with TLS encryption, and the verification service keeps no copy of it. Your video files, playback history, and preferences remain on your device; the local history database and the encrypted entitlement file are explicitly excluded from Android Auto Backup so they are not copied to the cloud.

7. Retention & deletion

  • Advertising/diagnostic data is retained according to Google's retention schedules (see Google's policy linked above).
  • Local data you control: clear watch history any time via the app's Settings → Clear History. Uninstalling the app deletes all local data.
  • Deletion requests: because we hold no server-side account data, email solusvoidofficial@gmail.com for privacy questions and we will direct you to the right Google control (Advertising ID reset/deletion, Play purchase records).

8. Remove Ads (one-time purchase)

The app offers a single one-time purchase, remove_ads_premium, via Google Play Billing. It permanently disables all ads and unlocks full resolution for high-resolution videos on the purchasing Google account. The entitlement is stored encrypted on your device (usable offline) and re-verified online through a stateless SolusVoid verification service, which validates the purchase with Google Play's Developer API and — on release builds — Google Play Integrity, in order to detect fake billing and tampered app copies. Refunds/revocations handled by Google Play remove the entitlement. Payment processing is handled entirely by Google Play under the Google Play Terms.

9. App permissions

Read media (video)To list and play your local video files. Required for the core feature.
Internet + network stateAds and purchase verification. Loading and displaying AdMob ads and consent messaging, plus the one-time Remove Ads verification (§8). Playback works without connectivity.
Advertising ID permissionTo read the resettable advertising ID for ads (see §4).
BillingTo offer and verify the one-time Remove Ads purchase. The verification step sends the purchase token (and a Play Integrity token on release builds) to the stateless SolusVoid service described in §8.
Keep screen on / wakeTo prevent the screen sleeping during playback.

10. Children

The app is a general-audience media player and is not directed at children under 13 as its primary audience. Ad content rating is declared through the Play content-rating questionnaire (which accounts for the presence of ads).
